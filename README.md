## Hendy Huayana

Environmental Engineer based in Lima, Peru. I work with geospatial data, remote sensing and climate risk.

At OEFA (Peru's environmental enforcement agency) I build risk assessments, early warning workflows and dashboards using satellite and climate data. Most of my work revolves around El Niño risk, hazard susceptibility, precipitation forecasts and environmental monitoring.

I'm also finishing my undergraduate thesis on heavy-metal removal from acid mine drainage, and I taught Geospatial Databases as a volunteer lecturer at UNFV.

**Tools I use:** ArcGIS Pro, ArcGIS Enterprise, QGIS, Google Earth Engine, Python (GeoPandas, Rasterio, ArcPy), SQL/PostGIS, Angular.

**Interests:** climate risk analytics, GeoAI, open environmental data.

I speak Spanish, Quechua and English. I'm part of Women in GIS, Women Techmakers and the Esri Young Professionals Network.

The projects here use open data only.
